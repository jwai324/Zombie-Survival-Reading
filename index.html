How it works

Three things to know

  1. The road. Aim with the mouse or your finger and hold to fire. Slide the van with the arrow keys, A and D, or the on-screen arrows. Walkers come from ahead and from both shoulders. Every one that reaches the van costs you.
  2. Safe havens. You are the convoy's reader. Someone hands you a radio log, a supply list, a letter, a warning. Read it, answer three questions, then decide which road to take. Right answers pay scrap. Reading pays better than shooting.
  3. The garage. Spend scrap on the van (armor, engine, ram plow, paint) and the gun (heavier rounds, rate of fire, spread), or a field repair.

There is no ending. The road goes on until the van stops. The score that matters is miles.

Pause with P or Esc. Home saves your exact spot; Continue brings you back to it.
